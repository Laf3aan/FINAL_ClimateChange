What is Climate Change?

Climate change is a long-term shift in Earth's average temperatures and weather conditions.

This is a major problem to the world. Why?

Why is Climate Change bad?

Human Health and Lives ❤️

1. 13 million deahs: Environmental factors already take around 13 million lives globally each year.
2. 250,000 extra deaths: The World Health Organization (WHO) estimates climate change will cause 250,000 additional deaths per year between 2030 and 2050 from heat stress, undernutrition, and diseases like malaria.
3. $2-4 billion in costs: Direct health damage costs will reach up to $4 billion per year by 2030.
4.6 billion people: This large share of the global population already lives in areas highly vulnerable to climate impacts.

Temperature and Weather ⛈️

5. 4°C hotter: Global average temperatures are now about 1.4°C (2.5°F) above pre-industrial levels, driving intense heatwaves.
6. Stronger storms: Warmer oceans feed heavier rainfall and more destructive hurricanes.

Water and Sea Levels 🌊

7. 2 Billion People: Over two billion people currently lack safe drinking water, a crisis worsened by changing rain patterns and droughts.
8. 0.5% fresh water: Only 0.5% of Earth's water is usable freshwater, and climate shifts put this tiny supply at risk.
9. 8 inches higher: Global sea levels have risen by about 8 inches (20 cm) over the last century, threatening coastal communities via data from NASA Science.
10. 30% carbon absorption: Oceans absorb about 30% of human carbon dioxide emissions, causing ocean acidification that damages marine life and coral reefs.

What is the cause of Climate Change?

Main Human Causes ⚒️

1. Burning Fossil Fuels: Using coal, oil, and gas for electricity, heat, and transport releases massive amounts of carbon dioxide.
2. Deforestation: Cutting down or burning forests releases stored carbon and destroys the trees that naturally absorb carbon dioxide.
3. Agriculture and Livestock: Raising cattle and sheep produces methane, while nitrogen fertilizers release nitrous oxide. Both gases trap heat intensely.
4. Industrial Manufacturing: Making goods like steel, cement, plastics, and electronics burns fossil fuels and releases industrial gases.
5. Waste and Landfills: Decomposing trash in landfills and wastewater treatment release methane gas.

Natural Factors 🌳

6. Volcanic Eruptions: Volcanoes release carbon dioxide, though their impact is small compared to human emissions.
7. Solar and Orbital Changes: Shifts in the earth's orbit and solar energy have shaped past climate cycles.

Yeah, we are the main problem of Climate Change.
